In short

  • Setlists.md doesn't come with a song library. Each church's library starts empty and holds only what its own team adds.
  • Your church is responsible for having the right to use the songs it adds, such as a CCLI licence where one applies, or permission from the author.
  • Songs are only visible to members of that church, and to people with a guest link for a single service.

Report a song that shouldn't be here

If you hold the rights to a song and think it's on Setlists.md without permission, email legal@setlists.md with:

  1. Your name and contact details, and who you act for if it's not you.
  2. The song (title and author) and, if you have it, a link or the church's name.
  3. Why you believe it's used without permission.
  4. A statement that the information is accurate and given in good faith.

We confirm we got your report within 2 working days, look at it, and remove or restrict the song if it's clearly not allowed. We tell the church what was removed and why.

If your church's song was removed

A church leader can reply to our email, or write to legal@setlists.md, if they believe the song is used with permission. We review it again and tell both sides the result. A church that repeatedly adds songs it has no right to may have its account suspended.

Our own name and design

The Setlists.md name, logo and app design belong to Setlists.md. Your songs and setlists stay yours.
