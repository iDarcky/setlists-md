Privacy

Beta version, last updated 9 November 2026. We will update this text before paid plans start and tell every account by email.

In short

  • We keep your name, email and what you play, so your team can plan with you.
  • Your church controls its own songs, setlists and members. We store them for the church and never sell them or use them for ads.
  • You can export your songs or ask us to delete your account at any time.

Who we are

Setlists.md is run by Daniel, based in Romania. Contact for anything about your data: privacy@setlists.md.

Two roles

Your account (name, email, password, settings): we decide how this is used, so we are the controller.

Your church's content (songs, setlists, schedules, who plays what): the church decides what goes in and who sees it. The church is the controller and we process it on the church's behalf.

What we store

  • Account: name, email, a hashed password, profile picture if you add one, your app settings.
  • Team: which churches or bands you belong to, your role, the instruments you play, when you're available, your answers to "Can you play?".
  • Content: songs, charts, setlists, notes and the history of changes, as you and your team enter them.
  • Technical: error reports when the app crashes (to fix bugs) and, if you turn them on, the push notification address of your device.

We don't use advertising trackers. The website does not set cookies. The app stores your library on your device so it works offline.

Why we use it

  • To run the service you signed up for (contract).
  • To keep it secure and fix errors (legitimate interest).
  • To email you about your account, your church request and changes to these terms. No marketing emails without your OK.

Who else handles it

We use a few providers to run Setlists.md. They only process data on our instructions:

  • Supabase: database, accounts and file storage.
  • Vercel: hosting for the app and this website.
  • Sentry: error reports.
  • Google Fonts: the website's fonts. Your browser fetches them from Google.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU. Where they do, they use the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Your account: until you delete it. We then remove it within 30 days, and from backups within 90 days.
  • Church content: while the church uses Setlists.md. Deleted songs and setlists stay in the trash for 30 days so they can be restored.
  • Error reports: up to 90 days.

Teens on the team

People under 16 can only be added by a leader of their church, with a parent's or guardian's OK. We collect only what the team needs: name, what they play and when they're available.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Write to privacy@setlists.md and we answer within 30 days. For content your church controls, we will pass your request to the church's leaders. You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP, dataprotection.ro) or the authority where you live.
