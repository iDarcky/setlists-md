I got an invite link

Open the link on the phone or tablet you'll play from. Make a free account (or log in), and your leader gets a request to let you in. Once they approve it, the church's songs and setlists appear on your Home.

Start a church or band

Make a free account, then tap Ask to start a church and tell us its name and how many people play. We set it up and email you, usually within two days.

Add Setlists.md to your Home Screen

Setlists.md runs in the browser, so there's nothing to download from an app store. Adding it to your Home Screen opens it full screen, like an app, and keeps your charts available offline.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Open app.setlists.md in Safari and log in.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open app.setlists.md in Chrome and log in.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots, top right).
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen, then confirm.

On a laptop, Chrome and Edge show an install button at the right of the address bar. It works fine in a normal tab too.
