# Terms of Service

> **The live Terms of Service are maintained in [`src/data/terms.md`](../../src/data/terms.md).**
>
> That file is the single source of truth — the app imports and renders it
> directly on the in-app Terms screen, so editing it updates what users actually
> see. This pointer exists so anyone browsing the repo's docs can find the legal
> documents in one place (`docs/legal/`) without the copy silently drifting out
> of sync with the app.

To change the Terms, edit `src/data/terms.md` and bump the "Last updated" date
at the top.
