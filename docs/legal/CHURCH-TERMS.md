# Church and band terms (data processing)

*Beta version. Last updated: 19 November 2026.*

These terms apply when you create a group on Setlists.md for a church, band or other organisation. You accept them for that organisation when you create the group. They describe how we handle your members' personal data for you, as GDPR article 28 requires.

## Roles

- **Your organisation** decides why and how members' data is used. It is the *controller*.
- **We** (Daniel Maghiș, Romania, hello@setlists.md) store and process that data only to run Setlists.md for you. We are the *processor*.

## What we process for you

- **Whose data:** your members and leaders, and guests you share links with.
- **What data:** names, emails, profile photos, roles, instruments, availability, replies to "who can play?", and anything members write in songs, setlists, notes and comments.
- **What for:** to store, sync and show your group's content to the people you allow, send the notifications and emails your group triggers, and keep the service secure.
- **For how long:** while your group exists, then as described under *When you leave*.

## What you agree to

- You have a lawful reason to keep your members' data in Setlists.md. For most churches and bands, that's legitimate interest in organising your ministry or band.
- You tell your members that you use Setlists.md and point them to our Privacy Policy.
- You don't add children under 13, and you have a parent's or guardian's OK before approving members aged 13 to 15, and you keep only what's needed about them (name, instrument, availability).
- You don't put sensitive data in the app, such as health information, pastoral or counselling notes, or anything beyond what planning music needs.
- You answer your members' privacy requests about group content. We help when you ask.

## What we promise

- We use your group's data only to run Setlists.md for you, following your instructions as given through the app's settings and these terms. We never use it for our own purposes, ads, or AI training.
- Anyone who works on Setlists.md with access to your data is bound to keep it confidential.
- We protect it with suitable security: encryption in transit, hashed passwords, per-group access rules, and limited admin access.
- We use only the providers listed in the Privacy Policy. Before adding or replacing one, we update that list and email group owners at least 30 days ahead. If you object and we can't solve it, you can close your group and export your content first.
- If a breach affects your group's data, we tell the owner without undue delay (we aim for within 48 hours) with what we know, so you can meet your own duties.
- We help you answer member requests (access, correction, deletion, export) and, where needed, with impact assessments or questions from a data protection authority.
- If you ask, we give you the information you need to check that we keep these promises.
- Where a provider handles data outside the EU, it relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

## When you leave

You can export your songs at any time. When a group is deleted, its content is deleted from the live database, and backup copies are overwritten within 90 days, unless the law requires us to keep something longer.

## Everything else

The Terms of Service apply to everything these terms don't cover. If the two conflict on how personal data is handled, these terms win.
