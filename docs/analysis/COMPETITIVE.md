# Competitive Positioning: vs. Planning Center

Planning Center Online (PCO) is the industry standard for large churches — but
it's built for **administrators**, not **musicians**. That gap is where
Setlists MD wins. Use PCO to schedule the team and plan the service; use
Setlists MD on the music stand.

---

## 1. The Five Core Advantages

### A. Data Ownership vs. The Walled Garden
- **PCO:** Charts live in PCO's proprietary database. Cancel your subscription
  and you lose seamless access; exporting means downloading hundreds of static
  PDFs.
- **Setlists MD:** Songs are `.md` files you own — on your device or in your own
  Google Drive. Even if Setlists MD disappeared, your files are readable plain
  text. Never locked in.

### B. Dynamic Rendering vs. Static PDFs
- **PCO:** Relies on PDFs. Changing key means logging in, transposing,
  regenerating the PDF, and everyone refreshing.
- **Setlists MD:** Charts render dynamically on-device. Transpose, toggle
  Nashville Numbers, or add a capo instantly with one tap — no server round-trip.

### C. Offline Reliability vs. Cloud Dependency
- **PCO:** Fundamentally cloud-based; struggles when church Wi-Fi drops.
- **Setlists MD:** Offline-first. Full functionality at gigabit or zero signal.

### D. Frictionless Guest Access vs. Account Management
- **PCO:** A musician needs an account, the right schedule slot, and permissions
  just to view a chart. Painful for guests and substitutes.
- **Setlists MD:** Send a guest bassist a zip or a link — they open it and play.
  No account required.

### E. Mid-Song Modulation & Musician Tools
- **PCO:** No native mid-song key changes in a single chart.
- **Setlists MD:** First-class `{modulate: +2}` markers, a built-in capo
  calculator, and personal inline notes that don't touch the master chart.

## 2. Comparison Table

| Feature | Setlists MD | Planning Center (Music Stand) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary focus** | Performance & rendering | Administration & scheduling |
| **Data format** | Open Markdown (`.md`) | Proprietary DB / PDF |
| **Data ownership** | 100% yours | Locked in PCO |
| **Offline** | Full | Partial (cached viewing) |
| **Instant transpose** | Yes (on-device) | Yes (requires re-generation) |
| **Guest access** | Scan/link, no account | Login required for all |
| **Modulation** | Native `{modulate}` | Manual / multi-file |
| **Nashville numbers** | One-tap toggle | Rare / hard to set up |
| **Cost** | Free core (paid sync) | Paid subscription |

## 3. Marketing Angles

1. **"The Small Church Hero"** — PCO is overkill for a 3-person team.
   *"Planning Center is for the office. Setlists MD is for the stage."*
2. **"Musicians First, Admins Second"** — *"Charts that move with you.
   Transpose, modulate, and switch to Nashville Numbers with one tap."*
3. **"Own Your Ministry's Assets"** — *"Your songs are your files. Hosted in
   your Drive. Accessible forever."*
4. **"Zero-Friction Guest Onboarding"** — *"Scan. Play. Done. No accounts for
   guests."*
