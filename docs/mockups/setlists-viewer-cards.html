Design decisions — (1) The identity card leads with the two jobs people open a set for: Play Live (brand) and Practice, with Edit/Export/Share/⋯ as quiet icons; date · time-range · duration · status sit on the subtitle line. (2) The set card shows each song with its structure code map + per-song cue note + key + length, and breaks render as distinct amber slides. (3) An optional roster/notes card sits alongside on desktop (who's playing + availability + shared notes), dropping below on mobile.
Open questions: show the roster card to everyone or only team/leaders? · tap a song row to open it in the hub (read) vs. expand inline? · keep "Edited by / Set key" pills, or trim to stay clean? · breaks as full amber slides here too, matching Live?
