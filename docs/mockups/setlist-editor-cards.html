Design decisions — (1) An identity card carries name · date · start/end · location · status (Draft/Ready), with the new end-time field inline. (2) The Set card is the ordered list of song rows + break rows (drag ⠿, key chip, duration, per-song note/transpose), with the running total in its header. (3) A Library card sits beside it (search + Recommended-next), folding into a bottom sheet on mobile behind "＋ Add songs". Three cards on desktop, stacked on mobile.
Open questions: a third "details" pane (roster/availability/private notes) as a fourth card, or keep that out of the editor? · per-song transpose shown as the key chip (tap to change) — enough, or expose a dedicated control? · Save in the header (shown) vs a sticky bottom bar (shown on mobile) — pick one across both?
